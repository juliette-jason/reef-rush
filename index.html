          Your Fishing Story

          You are a fisher bringing fresh catches back to the store. Fill your net with valuable fish, earn coins, and buy better gear, but beware: the deadly kraken prowls the water and can steal fish from your catch.

          How to Play

          • Move left and right to aim your line.
          • Press Enter to cast deep through the water.
          • Press Space, tap, or lift to snag fish near the hook.
          • Avoid catching the kraken, or it can rip your net and cost you fish.
          • Score well to earn coins for the Fishing shop.

          Fish Rarity

          Fish can be Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, or Legendary. Rarer fish are harder to find, but they are worth much more for your score.
